To judge live: the torso yaw is not measured (the torso reads square to the camera), the One Euro constants are the body solver's, and on the webcam take the single view's palm frames disagree with the stereo ones (the gate tool's input lines): the left hand's by the lift's depth mirror, which the solver undoes by the forearm (65 deg p50 remains), the right hand's by about 146 deg that neither the palm nor its mirror explains, and the raw finger angles 27 deg apart on both, which the hand lift rather than the solver would have to answer.
- [ ] Optional intrinsics in the direct setup: the single-front chain's board step gets a Skip with a warning (a nominal focal length scales the shoulder depth, and so the arm, by the camera's true field of view against the guess, a few degrees of direction at the frame edges), and skipping seeds the camera profile with default intrinsics marked nominal, so the Calibration panel still offers the wizard and the direct solve no longer gates on `intrinsics.present` alone. The defaults come from a shipped webcam table keyed by USB vendor id, product id and resolution, matched against the device path (which carries the ids) with a same-aspect scaling rule, starting from the FTC SDK's calibrations at https://github.com/FIRST-Tech-Challenge/FtcRobotController/blob/master/TeamCode/src/main/res/xml/teamwebcamcalibrations.xml (Logitech C270, C310, C920 at several sizes, Microsoft LifeCam HD-3000: `size`, `focalLength`, `principalPoint`, eight `distortionCoefficients`), with a frame-size-and-assumed-FOV pinhole as the last resort for an unlisted camera.
